## 0.2-alpha.0

- Constructive mathematical formulation: open observations may lead to new mathematical questions without a pre-specified target claim.
- 建设性数学构造：允许从开放观察形成数学问题，无需预先给出 target claim。
- Task-relevant productive derivation delivers feasible conditional results instead of only diagnosing claim status. Provisional modeling stays conditional; no unrestricted theorem-proving service is introduced.
- 任务相关建设性推导交付可行条件性成果，不再仅诊断 claim status；暂定模型保持条件性，不扩展为无限制定理证明服务。
- Revised stopping contract: deliver the supported result and conditions, or stop at a specific blocker; unresolved physical identification alone does not block feasible mathematics.
- 修订停止契约：交付有依据的成果及条件，或遇具体障碍停止；物理机制未识别本身不阻断可行数学工作。
- Conditional verification guidance uses authorized tools when a consequential check has material benefit; numerical sampling is not a general proof.
- 条件性核查指引仅在关键核查有增益时使用已授权工具；数值抽样不是一般性证明。
- Frontmatter compatibility: concise YAML mapping and exact version/title alignment. The available alpha.2 frontmatter already parsed as YAML; no invalid standalone Chinese field was found in that file.
- Frontmatter 兼容性：简短 YAML mapping 与版本/标题一致。本地 alpha.2 已能解析，未发现该文件含非法独立中文字段。
- Four Core rules, OAC-1–6 and fifteen Prohibited Defaults preserved byte-for-byte; historical evidence and tags unchanged. This is an explicitly authorized capability upgrade, not a confirmed Skill failure repair.
- 四条 Core、OAC-1–6 与十五项 Prohibited Defaults 逐字节保留；历史证据与标签不变。这是明确授权的能力升级，不是已确认 Skill failure repair。
- Evidence limitations: available historical fixtures and targeted cases are not comprehensive reliability evidence. Earlier Batch1 original prompts are missing from the accessible archive; preserve its historical PASS rather than relabel it as a current pass. Small same-model comparisons cannot establish stable incremental benefit.
- 证据限制：可取得的历史输入和定向案例不是全面可靠性证据。较早 Batch1 原始输入缺失，保留历史 PASS 而不重标为本次通过；小规模同模型对照不能证明稳定增量价值。

- Validation: available historical cases 3/3 and new branches 6/6 passed; native full loading passed. Two B/C/D tasks did not show a necessary-result advantage. Model tool execution remains unverified in the blocked nested environment. See [validation record](docs/v0.2-alpha.0-validation.md).
- 验证：可取得的历史案例3/3、新分支6/6及原生完整加载通过；两个B/C/D任务未显示必要成果优势，受阻嵌套环境中模型工具执行仍未验证。详见[验证记录](docs/v0.2-alpha.0-validation.md)。

## 0.1-alpha.2

- Clarified stopping guidance: completing epistemic diagnosis does not end a formulation task when a warranted, bounded, claim-relevant operational step is still required. Stopping is preserved when further progress requires unsupported commitments. Motivated by targeted value testing and replication.
- 澄清停止指引：如果仍需要一个有依据、有界且与 claim 相关的可操作步骤，不能仅因认识论诊断完成就结束表述任务。若进一步推进需要无依据的承诺，仍应停止。此变更由定向价值测试及复现结果推动。
