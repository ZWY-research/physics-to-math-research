## Capability change and scope / 能力变更与范围

This is the user's explicitly authorized production-capability design, not a claimed failure repair or measured capability gain. The smallest implementation replaces the existing derivation guidance with three independently callable capabilities, adds existing-tool and continuation-record guidance, and provides one on-demand reference. No new Core rule, general software wrapper, dependency or simulation workflow was added.
这是用户明确授权的生产能力设计，不称为已确认失败修复或实测能力增益。最小实现是将原推导指引改为三种可独立调用的能力，补充现有工具与续研记录指引，并提供一份按需参考。未增加 Core 规则、通用软件壳、依赖或模拟流程。

- Research Discovery: turn a scientific phenomenon into a researchable mathematical question and complete a feasible first construction or derivation.
- Mathematical Development: carry supplied equations or results to a conditional proposition, structural relation, bound or computational construction, using suitable existing tools.
- Research Continuation: inherit results and their premises, solve the next mathematical obstacle and retain an incremental research record.

- 问题发现：将科学现象转为可研究的数学问题，并完成可行的首项构造或推导。
- 数学推进：从已有方程或结果实际取得条件命题、结构关系、界或计算构造，按需使用合适的现有工具。
- 研究续接：继承成果与前提，解决下一数学障碍，留下增量研究记录。

The reference contains two executable examples: heat exchange to an invariant and decay law; weighted flux to a sharp sufficient monotonicity criterion, then an exact sign interval in a specified family. Both are analytic conditional models with illustrative parameters, not experiments or claims of mathematical novelty. Source influences are linked in the reference; no third-party skill text, code or runtime dependency was imported.
参考文件包含两个可执行示例：从热交换得到不变量和衰减律；从加权通量得到可达到的单调性充分判据，并在指定函数族中续接到精确符号区间。二者都是使用示意参数的解析条件模型，不是实验或数学原创性声明。设计来源在参考文件中链接；未引入第三方 Skill 文本、代码或运行依赖。

## Regression risk / 回归风险

Discovery could overproduce questions, continuation could inherit an unsupported result, and computation could displace needed analysis. The changed guidance ties each capability to the current scientific task and feasible outcomes, preserves inherited assumptions and support status, and retains existing claim-specific checks and simulation boundaries. The examples and section comparisons protect these concrete mathematical and textual properties; they do not establish general behavioral reliability. This PR is left unmerged for review.
发现能力可能过度生成问题，续研可能继承依据不足的结果，计算可能挤占必要解析。变更将能力限定于当前科学任务与可行成果，保留继承前提和支持状态，并保留既有命题级核查及模拟边界。示例与区段比较核对的是这些具体数学和文本性质，不能建立普遍行为可靠性。本 PR 留待审查，不自动合并。
