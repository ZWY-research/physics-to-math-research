C1 — Momentum Transport Identifiability 动量输运可识别性

Can observations separate diffusion, pinch and residual stress? Explicit shared-parameter assumptions yield rank conditions and error bounds; arbitrary residual changes permit non-unique decompositions. The same rotation profile can arise from different cancelling contributions. Omitted-term bias may be zero or have either sign; zero fit residual does not ensure correct identification.

观测能否分离扩散、pinch 与 residual stress？明确共享参数条件下得到秩条件及误差界；允许残余项任意变化则分解不唯一。同一旋转剖面可由不同贡献抵消实现。遗漏项偏差可为零，方向不固定；零拟合残差不保证正确识别。

Conditional Mathematical Review — Accepted with Limitations条件性数学审查：附限制接受

Least-squares formulas are unconstrained; positive estimated diffusion requires a separate constrained treatment. The C1 session had reference-review exposure.最小二乘公式为无约束估计；估计扩散严格为正需另作约束处理。C1 会话曾接触参考评议。

C2 — Density Gradient Self-Consistency 密度梯度自洽性

Does state-dependent particle transport admit a unique zero-flux density gradient? Conditional proofs give existence, uniqueness and root-perturbation bounds on a specified interval and state branch. Positive diffusion and inward non-diagonal drive still allow three distinct roots in a mathematical counterexample. A non-monotone peaking–frequency association does not prove multiple self-consistent roots under the same background.

状态依赖粒子输运是否存在唯一零通量密度梯度？条件性证明给出指定区间与状态分支上的存在性、唯一性和根扰动界。数学反例表明，正扩散与向内非对角驱动仍允许三个不同的根。非单调峰化—频率关联不能直接证明同一背景下存在自洽多解。

Conditional Mathematical Review — Accepted条件性数学审查：接受

Artificial closures are not validated ITG/TEM models. Root-perturbation bounds do not establish dynamical stability or actual tokamak multiplicity.人为闭合不是已验证的 ITG/TEM 模型；根扰动界不证明动力学稳定性或真实托卡马克多解。

No raw experimental reanalysis, full-paper validation, journal peer review or fully independent external refereeing is claimed. No credible Skill OFF control; Skill incremental gain: NOT ESTABLISHED. Physical closure and device validation remain open; These cases are not included in Reviewed Papers.未重新分析原始实验，不冒称全文验证、期刊同行评审或完全独立外审。没有可信 Skill OFF 对照，增量能力尚未证明。完整物理闭合与装置验证仍待完成；这些案例不计入已审查论文数量。

Fable et al. · 2010

Electron Density Transport and ITG/TEM Spectral Response 电子密度输运与 ITG/TEM 谱响应

Physical source: Plasma Physics and Controlled Fusion 52, 015007 (2010)

Research Question 研究问题

What determines the stationary electron-density gradient's conditional responses to electron and ion temperature gradients in ITG/TEM turbulence?

在 ITG/TEM 湍流背景下，定态电子密度梯度对电子温度梯度和离子温度梯度的条件响应由哪些因素决定？

Mathematical Progress 数学进展

  • Derived both conditional responses from the paper's quasi-linear flux decomposition.从原文准线性通量分解推导两种条件响应。
  • Separated direct temperature-gradient drive from modal-coefficient and spectral-weight feedback.区分直接温度梯度驱动、单模系数变化和谱权重反馈。
  • Proved sufficient response-ordering criteria with explicit margins, including conditions for the opposite ordering.证明带明确余量的排序充分条件及相反排序条件。
  • Constructed a mathematical counterexample with the same mean frequency but different response orderings.构造平均频率相同而响应排序可改变的数学反例。
  • Distinguished fixed-alpha and pressure-recomputed-alpha control protocols.区分固定 alpha 与按压力梯度重算 alpha 的控制协议。

Research objective / 科研目标

What This Skill Does 这个 Skill 做什么

Bring a scientific phenomenon, observations, a model or an open question. The Skill helps formulate general mathematical questions, complete feasible analysis and identify next mathematical steps for unresolved parts. No laboratory or simulation software is required; unverified physical applicability is labelled, with optional validation routes.

提供科学现象、观测、模型或未解决问题，Skill 帮助构造一般数学问题、实际推进可行分析，并给出未解决部分的下一数学动作。无需实验装置或模拟软件；未经验证的物理适用性须明确标注，可说明可选验证途径。

This is a research objective, not a mandatory reasoning pipeline or a guarantee of success.

这是项目的研究目标，不是强制推理流水线，也不是成功保证。
