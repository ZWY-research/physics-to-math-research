Source evidence & attribution 原文证据与贡献来源

Codex Stage A extracted five physical evidence items and five mathematical relations from the PDF. Some normalization and parameter definitions remain unresolved. The subsequent ChatGPT review accepted Stage A with limitations; it did not recheck the original PDF pages.

PDF 证据提取来自 Codex 阶段 A：五项物理证据、五项数学关系，部分归一化和参数定义尚待核实。后续 ChatGPT 评议对阶段 A 的内容判断为 ACCEPT WITH LIMITATIONS，未重新核查原始 PDF 页面。

Conditional mathematical results 条件性数学结果

The subsequent ChatGPT review records full-column-rank identifiability under explicit shared-parameter assumptions and least-squares parameter bias from an omitted residual term. The bias may vanish and has no fixed direction. These are conditional linear-algebra results, not Skill ON research achievements.

后续 ChatGPT 评议在明确共享参数假设下给出简化线性逆问题的满列秩可识别条件，以及遗漏 residual stress 的最小二乘参数偏差。偏差可能为零，方向不必固定；属于条件性线性代数结果，不是 Skill ON 科研成果。

No microscopic turbulent mechanism or complete original experimental dataset has been independently validated. Effective-parameter identification does not identify a unique mechanism. Skill ON independent research has not been performed; Skill incremental gain is not established. This case does not increase Reviewed Papers.未证明真实湍流微观机制，也未独立核实全部原始实验数据；有效参数识别不等于唯一机制识别。Skill ON 独立研究未执行，科研能力增量尚未证明；本案例不增加正式审查论文数。
